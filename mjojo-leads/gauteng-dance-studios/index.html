Why This Fits Gauteng Dance Studios

Gauteng Dance Studios inspires incredible creativity through dramatic arts, music, and design every single day. Our financial literacy activation will empower your talented learners with practical money skills to support their artistic journeys.

Optional workshops and facilitator support for deeper engagement.

About MJOJO Finance Play

MJOJO Finance Play has been bringing financial literacy to life through theatre since 2016, reaching more than 50 schools across South Africa with programmes that turn money lessons into performance, conversation, and practical action.
